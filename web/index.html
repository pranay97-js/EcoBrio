The 6-Stage Multi-Stream Cycle

How mixed urban waste streams become pristine circular materials without landfill degradation.

1 STAGE 01

Identify & Report

Citizen scans or drops any material stream using contactless QR reservation or KachraSeth AI natural language prompts.

2 STAGE 02

Reserve Smart Hopper

Distributed Redis key leases lock hub hopper capacity for guaranteed contactless drop-off without waiting queues.

3 STAGE 03

Multi-Spectral AI Triage

Hardware bin applies NIR optical spectrometry, inductive sensors, and precision tare load cells to verify purity inside seconds.

4 STAGE 04

Autonomous Segregated Transit

Electric multi-compartment vans dynamically route each material to specialized anaerobic digesters, pellet mills, or smelters.

5 STAGE 05

Instant Token Minting

Cryptographic tokens are minted immediately onto the citizen's verified ledger without gas fees or third-party brokers.

6 STAGE 06

Municipal Scope-3 Audit

City directors observe unified diversion metrics, methane reductions, and circular reuse certificates in real-time dashboards.
