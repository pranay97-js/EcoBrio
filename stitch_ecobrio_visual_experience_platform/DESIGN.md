## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, planetary-scale aesthetic dubbed **Eco-Futurism + 3D Spatial Intelligence**. It merges raw ecological stewardship with precision algorithmic infrastructure, transforming the perception of e-waste collection from transactional waste hauling into an advanced, data-dense reverse-logistics network.

### Visual Pillars
- **Planetary Precision:** Clear Cartesian structures, orbital nodes, and geospatial data meshes anchored by deep biosphere hues.
- **Luminescent Telemetry:** Emerald and electric lime bioluminescent accents against a midnight canvas, directing immediate visual focus toward spatial anomalies, route efficiencies, and asset recovery metrics.
- **Glassmorphic Stratification:** Translucent frosted overlays mirroring atmospheric layers, conveying elevation and spatial containment without visual clutter.

### Audience & Mood
Targeting enterprise supply-chain directors, urban logistics operators, and municipal sustainability heads. The interface projects high-credibility operational mastery, computational superiority, and ecological permanence.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark mode, establishing high-contrast readability against infinite telemetry maps and 3D geospatial overlays.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#071411`):** Deep Midnight Abyssal Green serving as the base layer, mitigating OLED power draw and eye fatigue during continuous control-room monitoring.
- **Primary Accent (`#00C897`):** High-frequency Emerald Green utilized for confirmed statuses, active collection points, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C7F464`):** Electric Lime serving as critical signal telemetry, highlighting AI optimization nodes, dynamic route changes, and urgent asset discoveries.
- **Structural Chroma (`#073D32`):** Deep Forest Green delivering grounding surfaces, base container fills, and low-priority zone groupings.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - **Text Primary (`#F7FAF7`):** Soft Mineral White for headers and critical metrics.
  - **Text Secondary (`#63736C`):** Subdued Slate for secondary descriptors, axes labels, and metadata.
  - **Surface Overlay (`rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.45)`): Glassmorphic plane with a 1px border tinted at `rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.25)`.

## Typography

Typography balances computational structure with high-legibility telemetry.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric clarity with soft curvature provides modern polish to industrial sustainability metrics. Display weights use negative tracking to form dense, commanding title blocks.
- **Body & Data Labels (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible at fine sizes, ideal for dense coordinate listings, hardware serial codes, and telemetry charts.
- **Tabular Numerics:** Numerical metrics, manifests, and coordinate trackers must strictly utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shift during live data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a flexible 12-column grid system bounded inside edge-to-edge geospatial canvases. 

### Breakpoints & Fluid Reflow
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column layout with 32px (`gutter-lg`) spacing and 48px (`margin-desktop`) padding. Side panels, spatial telemetry drawers, and orbital maps operate simultaneously.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`gutter`) spacing and 32px (`margin`) margins. Control panels collapse into contextual slide-overs or floating overlays above the central map view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px (`gutter-sm`) spacing and 16px (`margin-mobile`) margins. Geospatial view remains the base canvas; UI sheets dock to the bottom with gesture-based pull handles.

### Rhythm
Component interiors adhere to multiples of 4px. Structural grouping relies on `space-md` for standard internal card padding, stepping up to `space-xl` between decoupled dashboard segments.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial hierarchy is maintained through frosted glass planes and ambient chromatic halos rather than traditional black dropshadows.

### Atmospheric Glass Surfaces
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#071411`.
- **Level 1 (Docked Modules & Sidebars):** Background `rgba(7, 20, 17, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle bottom border `rgba(99, 115, 108, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Data Overlays):** Background `rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed by a 1px border in `rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Real-Time Alerts):** Background `rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, framed by a 1px border in `rgba(199, 244, 100, 0.4)`.

### Chromatic Light Emittance
- **Interactive Hover:** Card surfaces gain an internal ambient glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.15), 0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Critical AI Indicators:** Telemetry nodes and alerts emit radial flares using `0 0 24px rgba(199, 244, 100, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances aerodynamic curves with structural stability.

- **Base Components:** 8px (`0.5rem`) radius for buttons, text inputs, and segmented controls.
- **Panels & Telemetry Cards:** 16px (`1rem`) to 24px (`1.5rem`) radius, softening the technical density and maintaining an approachable, forward-looking aesthetic.
- **Orbital Accents:** Circular shapes (full 9999px pill or circle) reserved strictly for node indicators, tracking rings, agent status badges, and radar sweep motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Signal Green):** Background `#00C897`, text `#071411`, font-weight 600, padding `10px 20px`, roundedness 8px. Hover shifts to `#C7F464` with transition `200ms ease-out` and a slight glow `0 0 14px rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary (Atmospheric Glass):** Background `rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.5)`, text `#F7FAF7`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.3)`. Hover brightens border to `#00C897` and background to `rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.8)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#63736C`. Hover transitions text to `#F7FAF7`.

### Telemetry Cards
- Constructed with `rgba(7, 61, 50, 0.45)`, a 1px border `rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.25)`, and `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- Corner elements optionally carry tactical micro-accents: a 4px corner tick or thin coordinate label in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Background `rgba(7, 20, 17, 0.6)`, text `#F7FAF7`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 115, 108, 0.3)`, height 44px, padding horizontal 14px.
- Focus state updates border to `1px solid #00C897` and applies an ambient spread `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status (Optimal):** Background `rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.12)`, text `#00C897`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 200, 151, 0.3)`. Contains a pulsing 6px radial dot.
- **Status (AI Reroute / Caution):** Background `rgba(199, 244, 100, 0.12)`, text `#C7F464`, border `1px solid rgba(199, 244, 100, 0.35)`.
- Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `4px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18x18px. Border `1.5px solid rgba(99, 115, 108, 0.5)`.
- Selected state fills with `#00C897` featuring an internal dark icon or central dot `#071411`.

### Data Visualizations & Geospatial Overlays
- **Paths & Flow Vectors:** Thin animated paths utilizing `#00C897` fading to transparent tail heads.
- **Logistics Clusters:** Concentric, radar-style orbital rings indicating aggregation density with translucent fills `rgba(199, 244, 100, 0.1)`.
